## About the Series
**Attack on Titan** (*Shingeki no Kyojin*) is a *dark fantasy action* anime based on the manga by Hajime Isayama. Humanity lives inside giant walls to protect itself from man-eating giants called Titans.

> A story about freedom, sacrifice, and the cost of war.
